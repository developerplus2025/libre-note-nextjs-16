# First Play

Once your music is imported, you're ready to play your first track. This section explains how to start playback and use basic controls.

## Starting Playback

To begin playing music:

1. Go to your **Library**.
2. Find a song you'd like to play.
3. Double-click the song, or select it and press the **Play** button.

The song will begin playing immediately, and the **Now Playing** area will update with track details.

## Playback Controls

You can control playback using the control bar at the bottom of the app:

- **Play / Pause** – Start or stop the current track.
- **Next / Previous** – Skip to the next track or go back.
- **Seek Bar** – Drag the progress bar to jump to any point in the song.
- **Volume** – Adjust the volume using the slider.

Keyboard shortcuts can be used for all of these actions. You can customize them in [Settings > Keyboard Shortcuts](/settings-customization/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Playback Modes

The software supports various playback modes:

### Repeat

- **Repeat One**: Repeats the current track.
- **Repeat All**: Loops the entire playlist or library.

### Shuffle

When shuffle is enabled, the app will randomly select the next track to play.

You can toggle shuffle and repeat using the buttons next to the playback controls.

## Now Playing Section

This section displays:

- Current track name, artist, and album
- Album artwork (if available)
- Elapsed and remaining time

Clicking the album cover will open a detailed view of the track.

## Track Queue

The queue shows upcoming songs:

- You can reorder tracks in the queue.
- Drag tracks into the queue manually or right-click a song and choose **Add to Queue**.

## Troubleshooting

If playback doesn't work:

- Make sure your audio output is properly selected in **Settings > Audio Devices**.
- Try restarting the app if audio drivers were updated recently.
- Check that the track is not corrupted or empty.
