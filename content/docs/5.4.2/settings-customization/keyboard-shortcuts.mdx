# Keyboard Shortcuts

Speed up your workflow with customizable keyboard shortcuts.

## Viewing Current Shortcuts

1. Open **Settings** > **Shortcuts**.
2. Browse by category: playback, navigation, effects, etc.

## Common Shortcuts

- **Space** – Play/Pause
- **← / →** – Seek backward/forward
- **↑ / ↓** – Adjust volume
- **Ctrl + N** – New Playlist
- **Ctrl + F** – Search

## Customizing Shortcuts

To change a shortcut:

1. Click on the existing keybinding.
2. Press your new key combination.
3. Conflicts will be highlighted automatically.

## Reset or Export

- Click **Reset All** to revert to defaults.
- Click **Export Shortcuts** to back up your custom layout.
