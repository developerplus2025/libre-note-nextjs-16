# Advanced Usage

This section explores powerful and lesser-known capabilities of the software to help you unlock its full potential.

## Custom Keyboard Shortcuts
You can create or modify keyboard shortcuts for common actions:
- Go to **Settings > Shortcuts**
- Assign your preferred key combinations
- Save and test the new shortcuts

## Automation
Automate repetitive tasks, such as:
- Batch renaming audio files
- Applying preset effects to multiple tracks
- Scheduling playlist playback

## Multi-Device Sync
Keep your settings, playlists, and audio libraries consistent across devices by enabling **Cloud Sync**.

## Pro Tips
- Use high-performance audio drivers for low-latency playback.
- Combine automation with hotkeys for instant workflow boosts.
- Explore advanced equalizer settings for professional-grade audio control.
