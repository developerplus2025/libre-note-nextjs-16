# Plugin Support

Extend the capabilities of your music software with third-party plugins.

## Supported Formats

- **VST2 / VST3** (Windows & macOS)
- **AU (Audio Unit)** (macOS only)

## Installing Plugins

1. Place the plugin files in your system's plugin folder:
   - Windows: `C:\Program Files\VSTPlugins\`
   - macOS: `/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/`

2. In the software, go to **Settings** > **Plugins**.
3. Click **Scan for Plugins**.

## Managing Plugins

- Enable or disable plugins individually.
- Organize plugins into folders (e.g. EQ, Delay, Reverb).
- Blacklist unstable or incompatible plugins.

## Using Plugins in the App

1. Open the **Effects** panel.
2. Click **Add Plugin**.
3. Choose from the list of available plugins.
4. Adjust parameters in the plugin’s UI.
