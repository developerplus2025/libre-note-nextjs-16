# No Sound

If you're not hearing any audio from the software, follow these steps to identify and fix the issue.

## 1. Check Output Device

Go to **Settings** > **Audio Devices** and confirm that the correct output device is selected. Try switching to a different one if needed.

## 2. Verify System Volume

- Ensure your computer's volume is turned up.
- Make sure the software is not muted in your system mixer.

## 3. Test Other Applications

Check if audio works in other programs. If not, the problem may be with your device or drivers.

## 4. Restart the Software

Sometimes a simple restart will reinitialize the audio engine.

## 5. Driver Update

Update your audio drivers (especially for external sound cards or interfaces). You can download the latest version from the manufacturer’s website.

## Still Not Working?

If none of the above work, try:

- Disabling exclusive audio mode in your OS.
- Reinstalling the software.
- Contacting support with your log file.
