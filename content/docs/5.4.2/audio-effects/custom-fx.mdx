# Custom FX Chains

For advanced users, the software offers support for custom FX chains. This allows you to build personalized audio pipelines using built-in and third-party effects.

## Creating an FX Chain

1. Open **Audio Effects** > **FX Chains**.
2. Click **New Chain**.
3. Drag and drop effects into the chain from the effects browser.

Supported effects include:

- Equalizer
- Reverb
- Delay
- Compressor
- Limiter
- 3rd-party VST plugins (if supported)

## Chain Order & Routing

The order of effects matters:

- Effects are processed top to bottom.
- Drag effects to rearrange them.
- Toggle each effect on/off individually.

Use **splitters** or **parallel routing** for advanced configurations (if supported in your version).

## Saving and Loading Chains

- Click **Save Chain** to reuse configurations.
- Load saved chains from the dropdown menu.

## Use Cases

- Build a mastering chain for high-fidelity output.
- Design an experimental sound with heavy effects.
- Apply subtle enhancements for everyday listening.
