# Smart Playlists

Smart playlists dynamically update based on criteria you set—no need to manually manage them.

## Creating a Smart Playlist

1. Click **New Smart Playlist**.
2. Set one or more rules, such as:
   - Genre is “Lo-fi”
   - Play count is greater than 10
   - Added within the last 30 days
3. Click **Save** and name the playlist.

It will automatically update whenever songs match the criteria.

## Available Filters

- Genre
- Artist
- Year
- Date Added
- Play Count
- Rating (if enabled)

## Examples

- **Recent Additions**: Songs added in the last 2 weeks
- **Top Tracks**: Play count > 20
- **Chill Mix**: Genre is “Ambient” or “Lo-fi”

Smart playlists are great for automation, especially when your library grows large.
