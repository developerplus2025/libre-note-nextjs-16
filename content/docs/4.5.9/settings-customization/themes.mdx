# Themes

Personalize your experience by switching between light and dark themes, or selecting a custom color palette that matches your mood or environment.

## Available Themes

- **Light Mode** – Optimized for daytime use.
- **Dark Mode** – Ideal for nighttime or low-light environments.
- **Auto** – Automatically switches based on system settings.

## How to Change Themes

1. Go to **Settings** > **Appearance**.
2. Select your preferred theme from the dropdown.
3. Changes are applied instantly.

## Custom Accent Color

You can also select an accent color for buttons, sliders, and highlights.

1. In **Settings** > **Appearance**, click **Accent Color**.
2. Choose a preset or pick a custom color from the color picker.

## Reset to Default

Click **Reset Appearance Settings** to revert all theme changes to the original look.
