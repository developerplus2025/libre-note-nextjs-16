# Troubleshooting

## No Sound Output
- Check if your audio device is selected in **Settings > Audio Output**.
- Ensure your speakers or headphones are connected and powered on.

## Playback Stuttering
- Reduce audio quality in settings.
- Close background applications using high CPU.

## Library Not Updating
- Refresh the library from **Settings > Library**.
- Ensure the folder paths are correct.

## Crashes or Freezes
- Update the software to the latest version.
- If issues persist, contact support with log files.
