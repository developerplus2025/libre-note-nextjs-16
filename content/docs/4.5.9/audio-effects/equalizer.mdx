# Equalizer

The built-in equalizer allows you to adjust the frequency balance of your music. It’s a powerful tool for enhancing your listening experience or tailoring playback to your environment.

## Accessing the Equalizer

To open the equalizer:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Audio Effects**.
2. Select the **Equalizer** tab.

## Equalizer Interface

The equalizer features a multi-band graphical interface:

- **Frequency Bands**: Typically includes 5–10 bands (e.g., 60Hz, 250Hz, 1kHz, 4kHz, 16kHz).
- **Gain Sliders**: Drag up or down to boost or cut specific frequencies.
- **Preset Menu**: Choose from built-in presets like:
  - Bass Boost
  - Vocal Clarity
  - Classical
  - Rock
  - Flat (neutral setting)

## Custom Presets

You can create and save your own presets:

1. Adjust the sliders.
2. Click **Save Preset**.
3. Name your preset and confirm.

Presets are stored locally and can be imported/exported for sharing.

## Use Cases

- Reduce harsh treble in bright headphones.
- Enhance bass in small speakers.
- Isolate vocals or specific instruments.

Changes apply instantly, so you can hear the results in real time.
