# MIDI Integration

The software supports full MIDI integration, allowing you to control playback, effects, and parameters using external MIDI devices.

## Supported Devices

- MIDI keyboards
- MIDI pad controllers
- Control surfaces
- USB MIDI interfaces

## How to Connect

1. Plug in your MIDI device via USB.
2. Open **Settings** > **MIDI Devices**.
3. Your device should appear in the list. Click **Enable**.

## Mapping Controls

1. Navigate to **Settings** > **MIDI Mapping**.
2. Select a function (e.g. Play, Volume).
3. Press a button or knob on your device to map it.
4. Save your custom layout.

## Troubleshooting

- Ensure your device is connected before launching the software.
- Use **Refresh Devices** if it doesn’t appear.
- Some devices may require drivers or firmware updates.
