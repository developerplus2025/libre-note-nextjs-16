# Reverb & Delay

Reverb and delay effects simulate spatial environments and echo, creating depth and atmosphere in your playback.

## Enabling Reverb or Delay

To enable these effects:

1. Go to **Audio Effects** > **Reverb & Delay**.
2. Toggle **Reverb** or **Delay** on.
3. Adjust the effect parameters as needed.

## Reverb Settings

- **Room Size**: Simulates small to large environments.
- **Damping**: Controls how quickly the echo fades.
- **Mix**: Adjusts how much of the effect blends with the original signal.

## Delay Settings

- **Delay Time**: Sets how long before the echo plays.
- **Feedback**: Controls how many repeats occur.
- **Wet/Dry Mix**: Blends the effect with the dry signal.

## Creative Applications

- Add a concert hall feel to classical music.
- Create an ambient soundscape for electronic tracks.
- Soften vocals or instruments with light reverb.
