# Playlists

Playlists let you organize your music into custom collections for specific moods, events, or personal preferences.

## Creating a Playlist

To create a new playlist:

1. Open the **Playlists** section from the sidebar.
2. Click the **New Playlist** button.
3. Enter a name for your playlist.
4. Add songs by dragging them from your library into the playlist area.

Playlists are saved automatically and can be accessed anytime.

## Managing Songs in a Playlist

Once a playlist is created, you can:

- **Reorder tracks** by dragging them up or down.
- **Remove a song** by right-clicking and selecting **Remove from Playlist**.
- **Play the playlist** from the beginning by clicking the play icon at the top.

## Renaming or Deleting a Playlist

- To **rename**: Click the playlist name and enter a new one.
- To **delete**: Right-click the playlist in the sidebar and choose **Delete Playlist**.

Deleting a playlist does not remove the actual songs from your library.

## Smart Playlists

Smart Playlists are dynamically generated based on conditions you set.

To create one:

1. Go to **New Playlist > Smart Playlist**.
2. Add one or more rules, such as:
   - Genre is "Jazz"
   - Play count is greater than 10
   - Added in the last 30 days
3. Click **Create** to finish.

Smart Playlists update automatically as your library changes.

## Exporting a Playlist

You can export any playlist as a file:

- Open the playlist.
- Click the **Options** menu (⋮) and choose **Export**.
- Select the format (e.g., `.m3u`, `.json`) and save it to your computer.
