# Organizing Songs

Keeping your music organized helps you find and enjoy your content faster. The software includes built-in tools for sorting, grouping, and categorizing songs.

## Sorting Options

You can sort your library by:

- **Title**
- **Artist**
- **Album**
- **Genre**
- **Date Added**
- **Play Count**

Click on any column header to sort ascending/descending.

## Creating Folders or Collections

Group songs manually:

1. Click **New Collection** in the sidebar.
2. Name the collection.
3. Drag songs into the collection.

Collections do not duplicate files—they just act as virtual groupings.

## Starred or Favorite Tracks

You can mark songs as favorites:

- Right-click a song > **Mark as Favorite**
- Or click the ⭐ icon next to a track

These appear in your **Favorites** list automatically.

## Tips for Organization

- Use collections for albums or moods (e.g. “Rainy Day Jazz”)
- Create folders per genre or activity
- Use smart playlists for automatic organization (see next section)
